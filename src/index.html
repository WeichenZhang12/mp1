About

Systems that stay up

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I study distributed systems, databases and operating systems. Before Illinois I earned my BS in Computer Science at UC Davis.

Four internships have put me on both sides of the stack: an LLM-powered failure triage pipeline at Solidigm, IoT telemetry infrastructure at CITRIS, a high-throughput Node.js backend at Elile AI, and an ordering platform at UC Davis that forty thousand students actually used.

The thread running through all of it is latency and failure. Making a p99 number drop, keeping a cluster consistent when a node disappears, finding the cache layer that turns a slow page into a fast one — those are the problems I keep coming back to.
